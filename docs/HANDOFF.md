## The Problem

NGOs managing operations across multiple countries face:

- **Disconnected data** — Staff records scattered across spreadsheets, email, and paper files
- **Compliance blind spots** — No visibility into mandatory training completion or expiring certifications
- **Manual reporting** — Donor reports require days of data gathering from country offices
- **Onboarding delays** — New staff take weeks to get visibility into available training

---

## What This System Does

### 1. Global Staff Directory
Search and manage personnel across all field offices and countries. Instantly find anyone by name, position, department, or location.

### 2. Training Management
Create, manage, and track training programs — from mandatory security training to specialized technical courses. Track enrollment, progress, completion, and scores.

### 3. Compliance & Certification Tracking
Automated alerts for expiring certifications. Real-time visibility into mandatory training compliance rates. Never miss a renewal deadline.

### 4. Analytics & Reporting
Executive dashboards with key metrics. Staff distribution by department and country. Training completion rates by category. One-click report export for donors.

## How to Explore the Demo

1. **Start at the Dashboard** — See the big picture: staff count, training completion, compliance alerts
2. **Browse Staff Directory** — Click any staff card to see their full profile, training history, and certifications
3. **Explore Training Programs** — Click any program to see enrolled staff, scores, and completion rates
4. **Check Reports** — View charts for department distribution, geographic presence, and compliance
5. **Try the Search** — Type a name, country, or position in the top search bar
