The Problem We Solve
What challenges NGOs face without a centralized system

Current Pain Points

  • Staff records scattered across spreadsheets and email
  • No visibility into mandatory training completion
  • Certifications expire without warning
  • Donor reports take days to compile manually
  • New staff can't find available training

How HRMS Solves This

  • Centralized staff directory with instant search
  • Real-time training completion dashboards
  • Automated certification expiry alerts
  • One-click report export for donors
  • Self-service training catalog
